How these numbers are calculated
Stock availabilityAn outlet-SKU pair counts as available when its stock reaches the workbook threshold (1). Stock availability % is available pairs ÷ total pairs, so it is a simple in-stock rate.
DOS availabilityCoverage is scored per pair as min(1, stock ÷ (forecast ÷ 30 × DOS days)) — the Summary!L6 rule. A pair with enough stock for the whole window scores 1; a pair with half of it scores 0.5. DOS % is the average of those scores.
Counts vs rates"SKUs" and "Outlets" are row counts after filtering. A SKU code carried on more than one list (Core and KVI, for example) is one row per list, so row counts can exceed unique item codes. Rates are unaffected.
